# Provenance

Melody Matcher is research from **Linn Autoracing Excellence LLC**. This repository is a curated source snapshot prepared on **September 13, 2026**, with newly authored explanatory documentation. It begins with clean repository history and does not represent the complete development record.

The retained material consists of Python collection and representation utilities, aggregate-library modeling and experiment code, timestamped event collectors, coverage analysis, and an experimental browser/backend harness. The original development source was reviewed at a fixed revision. Personal data and private source-history identifiers are not part of this release.

Curation preserved research logic and preexisting defects. Module introductions for experimental scripts were rewritten to remove unsupported execution recipes; personal input assumptions and a personal-library-derived seed default were generalized or removed. Region-fill sampling requires explicit seeds. An unattributed Apple brand glyph was replaced with an XML text label, and an unsupported local-only upload assurance was corrected. These are publication changes, not evidence of functional repair or recommendation improvement.

Original READMEs and private planning records were not imported wholesale. Historical results in [Research](docs/RESEARCH.md) are attributed by date and explicitly separated from current code and proposed experiments. The reproduction artifacts for those reports are absent. The [component guide](docs/COMPONENTS.md) records known limitations rather than silently repairing them.

Excluded categories include personal listening/library exports, audio, model/data artifacts, private operational and collaboration records, old development history, vendored dependencies, caches, and duplicate design archives. Generic curated artist seeds and genre mappings remain as evidence of human sampling choices; they are not user listening records. The harness contains code-defined geometric controls and generated-color artwork placeholders, with no album-cover images or bundled fonts.

The company has authorized the [MIT grant](LICENSE) for its owned source and documentation. Preparation used AI-assisted curation and writing, with responsibility for final acceptance retained by the project owner.
