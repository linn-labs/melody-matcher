# Contributing

Melody Matcher is paused, unfinished research. Forks, critical analysis, and focused research contributions are welcome; active maintenance, response times, and operational support are not promised.

Read [Research](docs/RESEARCH.md), [Experiments](docs/EXPERIMENTS.md), and [Data and rights](docs/DATA_AND_RIGHTS.md) before proposing work. Distinguish implemented behavior, reproduced evidence, historical reports, and hypotheses. Describe the question, comparison, confounds, and evidence that would change the conclusion.

Keep contributions small enough to review. Describe source changes and validation honestly, including what was not run. Do not present a syntax check as runtime validation or an offline metric as recommendation satisfaction. Submissions must be yours to license under this repository's terms; preserve relevant third-party attribution.

Do not submit credentials, personal libraries, real listening records, audio, weights, derived artifacts with unresolved rights, or private operational details through issues, pull requests, logs, or attachments. Use synthetic examples. Do not post sensitive security details publicly; this project does not advertise a dedicated private security reporting service.

Collection, model downloads, training, and deployment require separate deliberate decisions and rights review. Historical scripts and proposed experiments are not automatic instructions to perform them.
